Treat the bloom. Spare the lake.

CyanoSelect is a free, open-source tool for pond and lake managers: compare algae bloom treatments by their risk to the rest of the lake, then see what one treatment is predicted to do in your own pond.

How to use it

Three steps, from the lab data to your pond.

  1. 1

    Compare treatments

    See which treatment leaves the widest margin for green algae, water fleas and fish, from published lab tests.

    Rank treatments →
  2. 2

    Simulate your pond

    Enter your pond, the weather and one treatment. See where the product goes and the predicted knock-down, toxin and oxygen over 14 days.

    Simulate your pond →
  3. 3

    Plan the treatment

    Where the project is heading: helping you choose a dose and an application method within the label. Today, try one plan at a time in the simulator and compare the results yourself.

Everything CyanoSelect shows is a model prediction from published lab data, not a treatment recommendation. Always follow the product label and your state's rules.

Model predictions, not a treatment recommendation. CyanoSelect ranks treatments from published lab data and simulates ponds; none of its predictions has been tested in a real pond yet. Registered products have undergone risk assessment by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, and the product label governs how they may be used. Follow the label and your state's rules, and consult a licensed aquatic applicator or your state agency before treating any water body.
