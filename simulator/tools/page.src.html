      The layered model follows one column of the treated water, with temperature from a daily heat budget. The map and side slice add the wind-driven currents and sideways mixing and track colonies one by one. They also carry toxin and oxygen, tilt the thermocline in wind, add the fast surface skin, and let injected dense liquids slide downhill. Not modeled yet: inflows, waves, the sloshing after the wind changes, oxygen in the cool bottom water, and colonies sinking by day. Known limits: in a strong wind, colonies that simply drift with the water can still gather up to about 10% too thickly along the downwind and upwind shores of the map, and in the side slice colonies at the very top move at the top level's average speed rather than the faster top centimetre. Nothing here has yet been compared with a measured pond: these are model predictions, not a treatment recommendation. Built from the CyanoSelect simulator blueprint (October 2026).
